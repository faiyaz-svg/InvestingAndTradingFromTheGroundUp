# Investing and Trading From the Ground Up

A self-study book on investing and trading US markets from Canada: how markets work, accounts and taxes, risk and position sizing, charts, swing and day trading, company financials, valuation, macro data, bonds, ETFs, futures, options, volatility, commodities, forex, REITs, crypto, market data, a Python quant track, market history and finance careers.

Read it at https://faiyaz-svg.github.io/InvestingAndTradingFromTheGroundUp/ or open the Markdown in `docs/` in VS Code.

For education only. Nothing here is financial, tax or legal advice. Price charts use made-up data.

Part of a series: [Math](https://faiyaz-svg.github.io/MathFromTheGroundUp/), [CS](https://faiyaz-svg.github.io/CSFromTheGroundUp/), [Music](https://faiyaz-svg.github.io/MusicFromTheGroundUp/) and [French](https://faiyaz-svg.github.io/FrenchFromTheGroundUp/) From the Ground Up.

## Building

```bash
py -3.14 -m venv .venv
.venv/Scripts/python -m pip install -r requirements-dev.txt
.venv/Scripts/zensical serve
```

`CLAUDE.md` lists the tools; `STYLE.md`, `LESSON_TEMPLATE.md` and `DESIGN.md` hold the writing, lesson and design rules.
