## Cheat sheet

### Formulas

| Formula | Symbols | Use |
|---|---|---|
| \( v(x) = x^{0.88} \) for gains, \( v(x) = -2.25(-x)^{0.88} \) for losses | \(x\) gain or loss from the reference point | Loss aversion (1992 median estimates) |
| \( \text{Risk} = \lvert E - S \rvert \times N \) | \(E\) entry, \(S\) stop, \(N\) shares | Planned loss in US$ |
| \( \text{P/L} = (X - E) \times N \times d - F \) | \(X\) exit, \(d\) +1 long or -1 short, \(F\) fees | Result after fees |
| \( R = \text{P/L} / \text{Risk} \) | | Result in multiples of planned risk |
| \( \text{Reward-to-risk} = (T - E)/(E - S) \) | \(T\) target, long trade | Check before entry |
| \( \text{SE} = s/\sqrt{n} \) | \(s\) standard deviation of R, \(n\) trades | How well your average R is known |

### Biases and fixes

| Bias | What it does | Rule that counters it |
|---|---|---|
| Loss aversion | A loss weighs about 2.25 times a gain | Measure in R, set the stop first |
| Disposition effect | Sells winners early, holds losers | Stop and target set before entry |
| Sunk cost | Entry price drives the decision | "Would I open this now?" |
| Anchoring | Entry price becomes the goal | Exit by chart and plan |
| Confirmation bias | Reads only agreeing evidence | Write one reason the trade could fail |
| Hindsight and outcome bias | Judges a decision by its result | Write Before fields first, review process apart from R |

### Pre-trade checklist

1. Named setup from my plan?
2. Entry, stop and target written?
3. Reward-to-risk at or above the plan minimum?
4. Size from the formula, risk at or under 1%?
5. Total open risk within the limit?
6. Under the daily loss limit, past any cooling-off pause?
7. Scheduled event before my exit?
8. One reason this trade could be wrong, written?

### Weekly review

1. Every trade logged, both stages.
2. Rule breaks counted, each with a reason.
3. Win rate, average win, average loss, expectancy and total R.
4. Split by setup and by plan-following.
5. At most one change, written as a rule.

### Paper and live rules

- Size from your real account. Add fees and slippage. A limit order fills only if the price trades through it.
- Go live when (the book's own rules of thumb, not tested standards): 30 or more paper trades of one setup, 90% or more followed the plan, all journaled, no loss worse than 1.2R, drawdown inside the plan.
- Live ramp (example, also untested): 0.25% risk for 20 trades, 0.5% for 20 trades, then plan size. Drop back to paper if the criteria fail.
- Change rules at the weekly review, not in the middle of a session.
