# Investing and Trading From the Ground Up

This book teaches investing and trading in US markets for a reader in Canada. It starts with how a market works and ends with options, futures, data and careers. Taxes, accounts and brokers are covered for Canadians (TFSA, RRSP, CAD and USD, CRA rules).

It has 22 parts and about 200 lessons. Each lesson is a 30 minute session: 15 minutes of reading, 5 of exercises and 10 of practice. Charts use made-up price data, so every example can be checked. The book is for education only. It is not financial, tax or legal advice.
