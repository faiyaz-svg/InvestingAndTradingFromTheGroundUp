# Reference

Look-up pages for the whole book. Each one summarises what the lessons teach and links back to the lesson, so the lesson stays the place to learn the idea.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [Glossary](glossary.md) | Every key term, with a link to the lesson that defines it |
| [Common mistakes](common-mistakes.md) | The mistakes each lesson names, grouped by part |
| [Formulas](formulas.md) | Every formula the book uses, with its symbols and lesson |
| [Financial ratios](ratios.md) | Ratios from company financials and valuation, what they tell you and their usual caveats |
| [Order types](order-types.md) | What each order type and time in force guarantees, and the extended-hours caution |
| [Economic calendar](economic-calendar.md) | US and Canadian releases with times in ET, plus the 2026 FOMC and Bank of Canada dates |
| [Options strategies](options-strategies.md) | Legs, limits, breakevens, Greeks and assignment risk for every strategy in part 15 |
| [Futures contract specs](futures-contract-specs.md) | Multiplier, tick, months, settlement and hours for ES, MES, NQ, MNQ, SXF and SXM |
| [Canadian tax quick reference](canadian-tax-quick-reference.md) | Accounts, limits, withholding, capital gains, ACB, superficial loss, T1135, options and crypto notes |
| [Data sources](data-sources.md) | Free data sources, access rules and licence notes |
| [Case study timeline](case-study-timeline.md) | The crashes, blow-ups and scams the book studies, in date order, with the risk lesson from each |

Facts that change, such as limits, fees, specs and dates, are marked "as of 2026" with their source, as in the lesson they come from. Check the source before you act on one.
