# Case study timeline

The major market events the book studies, in date order, with what broke and the risk lesson from each. Each row links to its lesson.

| Date | Event | What broke | Risk lesson | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| October 1929 | 1929 crash | Leverage. Buyers put down about 10% and borrowed the rest, so a small fall forced sales. | Size so that no fall forces a sale. A loan of 90% is wiped out by a 10% fall. | [1929 and 1987](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/01-1929-and-1987.md) |
| October 19, 1987 | 1987 crash | Portfolio insurance, a rule that sold index futures as prices fell. | Rules that sell into a fall add to it. Stops cluster. | [1929 and 1987](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/01-1929-and-1987.md) |
| 1998 | LTCM collapse | Leverage of about 28 to 1. Lenders did not enforce their own risk standards. | Leverage turns a small spread move into a large loss of equity. | [LTCM and the dot-com bubble](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/02-ltcm-and-the-dot-com-bubble.md) |
| 1999 to 2001 | Dot-com bubble | Prices needed years of fast growth, and research was not independent. | Ask what the price assumes. Check who is paid when you buy. | [LTCM and the dot-com bubble](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/02-ltcm-and-the-dot-com-bubble.md) |
| 2007 to 2009 | 2008 financial crisis | Leverage as high as 40 to 1 at five large investment banks in 2007, short-term funding, and failures such as Lehman Brothers on September 15, 2008. | Funding and counterparty risk. Assets that move together on one driver. | [The 2008 financial crisis](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/03-the-2008-crisis.md) |
| May 6, 2010 | Flash crash | Liquidity vanished for about 20 minutes. A large sale ran by volume, not price, and some trades filled at stub quotes from a penny to US$100,000. | Use limit orders in fast markets. Expect worse fills. | [The 2010 flash crash and the 2020 COVID crash](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/04-flash-crash-2010-and-covid-2020.md) |
| February 2014 | Mt. Gox | Withdrawals were halted and the exchange filed for bankruptcy. A June 2023 indictment alleges that about 647,000 bitcoin was taken. | Custody. Customers held a claim on the exchange, not their own keys. | [Hacks and collapses](../18-crypto/08-hacks-and-collapses.md) |
| March 2020 | COVID crash | Liquidity thinned even in US Treasuries ("dash for cash"). Market-wide circuit breakers halted trading four times in March. | Safe assets can fall for a few days. Circuit breakers pause trading and do not stop the fall. | [The 2010 flash crash and the 2020 COVID crash](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/04-flash-crash-2010-and-covid-2020.md) |
| January 2021 | GameStop and meme stocks | Short interest of 122.97% of float, and large clearing margin calls (US$6.9 billion on January 27). Some brokers restricted trading. | A short has no loss limit. Brokers can change their rules in a day. | [GameStop and meme stocks](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/05-gamestop-and-meme-stocks.md) |
| March 2021 | Archegos | Total return swaps gave exposure of US$160 billion on US$36 billion of value at the peak. Price falls in concentrated positions triggered margin calls the firm could not meet. | Exposure divided by capital is the number to know. | [Archegos and SVB](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/06-archegos-and-svb.md) |
| March to December 2022 | 2022 rate shock | The Fed raised its target range to 4 1/4 to 4 1/2 percent. The 10-year Treasury yield rose from 1.52% to 3.88%, and bond prices fell. | List what in your accounts is rate-sensitive. | [The 2022 rate shock](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/07-the-2022-rate-shock.md) |
| May 2022 | Terra and Luna | UST, an algorithmic stablecoin with no dollar reserve, lost its peg. The SEC says about US$40 billion of market value was wiped out. | Ask what backs the token: reserves, or a rule that needs buyers to keep it up. | [Hacks and collapses](../18-crypto/08-hacks-and-collapses.md) |
| November 2022 | FTX | Prosecutors said customer money was used to prop up Alameda, the founder's trading firm. The founder was sentenced to 25 years on March 28, 2024. | Counterparty risk. A balance on an exchange screen is a promise. | [Hacks and collapses](../18-crypto/08-hacks-and-collapses.md) |
| March 2023 | SVB | About 94% of deposits were uninsured at the end of 2022. Held-to-maturity bonds had a duration of 6.2 years. Depositors withdrew over US$40 billion on March 9. | Safe bonds still have price risk. Check where your cash is held and who protects it. | [Archegos and SVB](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/06-archegos-and-svb.md) |
| No single date | Pump and dumps, finfluencers and signal groups | Promoters sell into the buying that their followers create. Win rates are shown without sample size, payoff or audit. | Ask who profits if you buy. Test a win rate against its sample size and payoff. | [Pump and dumps, finfluencers and signal groups](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/08-pump-and-dumps-finfluencers-and-signal-groups.md) |
| No single date | Prop firms and checking registration | An evaluation fee is paid whether or not you pass. CIPF covers property at member firms, not market losses. | Work out the cost per pass (fee divided by the pass rate) and check registration before you pay. | [Prop firms and checking registration](../21-crashes-scams-and-case-studies/09-prop-firms-and-checking-registration.md) |
