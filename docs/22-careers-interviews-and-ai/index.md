## Cheat sheet

### Finance jobs by whose money it is

| Group | Roles | Paid by |
|---|---|---|
| Sell side | Investment banking, sales and trading, equity research | Fees, commissions, spreads |
| Buy side | Asset managers, pension funds, hedge funds | Share of assets (and profit) |
| Prop trading | Quant researcher, trader, developer | Own capital's profit |
| Other | Wealth management, risk, fintech | Fees or salary |

### Credentials (as of 2026, confirm on the official page)

| Credential | What it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CIRO registration | Licence to advise or trade at an investment dealer | Dealer sponsors; nine-exam model from January 1, 2026; exams replace the CSC as the path for new candidates (confirm on CIRO's site) |
| CFA | Three exam levels, 4,000 hours of work over 3 or more years | Level I or higher exempts three CIRO exams for 3 years |
| FRM | Risk certification (GARP), two parts | Two years of risk work for certification |
| CMT | Technical analysis (CMT Association) | Check the association's site |

### Formulas

| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| Standard error of a mean | \( s / \sqrt{n} \); 95% range is mean plus or minus 1.96 x SE |
| Expected value | \( \sum x_i p_i \) |
| Bayes' rule | \( P(A \mid B) = P(B \mid A) P(A) / P(B) \) |
| Enterprise value | Equity value + debt + preferred + minority interest - cash |
| At least one false positive in \(k\) tests at 5% | \( 1 - 0.95^k \) |
| Rule of 72 | Years to double = 72 / rate in percent |

### Checklists

Spoken pitch: thesis, variant view, catalysts, valuation range, risks, what would change my mind, under 2 minutes.

Market walkthrough: stocks, rates, dollar, commodities, volatility, one event, what is next (with a date).

Depreciation up by D at tax rate t: net income falls by D(1 - t), cash from operations rises by tD, PP&E falls by D, equity falls by D(1 - t).

AI summary check: supply the document, find each number in it, recompute each ratio, check the statement links, log what you checked.

Never paste account numbers, a SIN, statements, client data or material non-public information into an AI service.
