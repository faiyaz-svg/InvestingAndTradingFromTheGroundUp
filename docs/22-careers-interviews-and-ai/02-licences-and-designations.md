After this lesson you can say which credentials a finance role needs, what the CFA program involves, and when you can skip them.

## The idea

You want to advise clients on stocks at a Canadian bank. A friend says "get the Canadian Securities Course." A year later you find the rules changed. Credentials in finance come from two different places, and mixing them up wastes months.

A **licence** (or registration) is permission from a regulator to do a regulated job, such as advising clients or trading for them. Without it, the work is illegal. A **designation** is a credential from a professional body, such as the CFA charter. It is not required by law, but employers use it to judge skills.

Facts on this page are as of 2026. Rules and exams change, so confirm each one on the official page linked here before you spend money.

## How it works

### Licences: CIRO

**CIRO** is the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization, the self-regulatory body for investment dealers and their registered staff in Canada. A person who advises clients or trades for a dealer must be registered as an Approved Person. A dealer sponsors the candidate: you cannot apply on your own.

CIRO introduced a new proficiency model on January 1, 2026. It replaces mandatory courses with exams for each category of registration, nine exams in all, delivered with Fitch Learning ([Investment Executive](https://www.investmentexecutive.com/from-the-regulators/heres-what-you-need-to-know-about-ciros-new-proficiency-model/); see CIRO's [exam hub](https://www.ciro.ca/registered-individuals/proficiency/exam-hub) for the current list). The first is the Canadian Investment Regulatory Exam (CIRE). New registrants must also complete code of conduct training within 30 days of approval.

The **Canadian Securities Course** (CSC) from the Canadian Securities Institute (CSI) used to be the standard first step. Under the new model, CIRO's exams are the requirement, not a course. Investment Executive reports that people registering under the old rules had to enrol in the courses by December 31, 2025, and then had a full year to finish them. That window closes at the end of 2026. CIRO's pages blocked our automated check, so confirm the transition rules on CIRO's site before you buy any course. The CSC still teaches useful basics, but do not buy it as a licence without checking.

The CFA Institute states that passing CFA Level I or higher exempts you from three CIRO exams (the CIRE, the Institutional Securities Exam and the Retail Securities Exam), valid for 3 years from your last CFA exam pass, and that your dealer applies for the exemption ([CFA Institute](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/cfa-program-ciro-exam-exemption-canada)).

### The CFA program

The **CFA** (Chartered Financial Analyst) program is run by the CFA Institute and has three levels, taken in order. The table is from the [CFA Institute exam page](https://www.cfainstitute.org/en/programs/cfa/exam) as of 2026.

| | Level I | Level II | Level III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Format | Multiple choice | Vignette-supported multiple choice | Vignette-supported essay and multiple choice |
| Length | 4.5 hours | 4.5 hours | 4.5 hours |
| Focus | Terms, concepts, formulas | Analyzing situations | Applying concepts to portfolios |
| 10-year average pass rate | 41% | 45% | 52% |

To earn the charter you must also pass all three exams, complete 4,000 hours of relevant work over at least 36 months, and become a CFA Institute member ([charter requirements](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/charter)). The curriculum topic areas include ethics, quantitative methods, economics, financial statement analysis, corporate issuers, equity, fixed income, derivatives, alternative investments and portfolio management, much of which this book teaches in parts 9 to 15. The curriculum outlines are on the CFA Institute site.

### Worked example: what the numbers mean

1. Hours per week. 4,000 hours over 3 years is 4,000 / 3 = 1,333 hours a year, or 1,333 / 52 = 25.6 hours a week of relevant work, so the charter takes at least three years of full-time work in the field.
2. The pass rates give a rough flow. Start with 1,000 Level I candidates: 1,000 x 0.41 = 410 pass. Of 410 at Level II, 410 x 0.45 = 184.5 pass. Of 184.5 at Level III, 184.5 x 0.52 = 95.9 pass. That is about 9.6 in 100.
3. This flow is rough. The averages mix first attempts and retakes, and people who reach Level III are a selected group, so do not read 9.6% as the chance you will finish.

</div>

### FRM and CMT, briefly

The **FRM** (Financial Risk Manager) is run by GARP and fits risk roles. It has two exam parts. GARP's [exam policies](https://www.garp.org/frm/exam-policies) require Part II within four years of passing Part I (by December 31 of the fourth year), and certification needs two years of relevant full-time work, which school internships do not count toward. The **CMT** (Chartered Market Technician) is run by the CMT Association and covers technical analysis. Check its site for requirements, since the part of this book on charts ([part 5](../05-reading-charts/index.md)) shows how technical analysis is tested.

### Which role needs what

| Role | Registration | Designation |
|---|---|---|
| Advising clients or trading at a dealer | CIRO Approved Person, sponsored by the dealer | CFA helps and can exempt exams |
| Investment banking, research | Research analysts have separate rules | CFA common, not required |
| Asset manager analyst | Often none for the analyst | CFA common |
| Risk | None | FRM common |
| Quant researcher, trader or developer | Usually none | Rarely asked; a degree and skills count |
| Fintech engineer | None | None |

This table is a pattern, not a rule for any one firm. A job posting states what the firm requires.

## Common mistakes

- Paying for a course because it was the standard path. Check the regulator's current page first.
- Starting the CFA with no job in the field. The exams are passable alone, but the charter needs the work hours.
- Assuming a credential beats a project. For quant and tech roles, a strong record of work (lesson 3) counts more.

## Try it

1\. You enrol in the CSC today to get a CIRO registration. What do the sources above say?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

As of 2026 CIRO registers new candidates through its own exams, not through the CSC. Investment Executive reports that people who enrolled in the old courses by December 31, 2025 had a year to finish. Check CIRO's exam hub for the exam you need.

</details>

2\. Of 2,000 Level I candidates, how many pass all three levels using the 10-year average pass rates in the table?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

2,000 x 0.41 x 0.45 x 0.52 = 191.9, about 192 people. It is a rough flow because it ignores retakes.

</details>

3\. A candidate works 35 hours a week, 48 weeks a year, all in qualifying work. How many months of work reach 4,000 hours?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

35 x 48 = 1,680 hours a year, so 4,000 / 1,680 = 2.38 years. The charter still needs at least three years, so the three-year minimum is the limit.

</details>

4\. Which credential would you ask a risk team about, and which a quant trading firm?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Risk: the FRM. Quant trading firm: ask what skills the interview tests, because designations are rarely required.

</details>

## Key terms

- **Licence (registration)**: permission from a regulator to do a regulated job.
- **Designation**: a credential from a professional body, not required by law.
- **CIRO**: the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization, which registers staff of investment dealers.
- **Approved Person**: a person registered with CIRO through a sponsoring dealer.
- **Canadian Securities Course (CSC)**: a CSI course that was the standard first step for registration.
- **CFA**: the Chartered Financial Analyst program, with three exam levels and a work requirement.
- **FRM**: the Financial Risk Manager certification from GARP.
- **CMT**: the Chartered Market Technician designation from the CMT Association.
