## Cheat sheet

### Formulas

| Name | Formula | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Surprise | \( S = A - C \) | Actual minus consensus, in the release's unit |
| Annualized rate | \( (1 + r)^{n} - 1 \) | \(n\) = 12 for a monthly change, 4 for a quarterly one |
| Unemployment rate | \( u = U / L \times 100 \) | \(U\) unemployed, \(L\) labour force |
| Implied odds of a cut | \( p = (R_0 - (100 - F)) / \Delta \) | \(F\) fed funds futures price, \(\Delta\) size of the cut |
| Present value | \( PV = \sum CF_t / (1+r)^t \) | Later cash is hit harder by a higher \(r\) |
| Diffusion index | \( D = P_{\text{higher}} + 0.5\,P_{\text{same}} \) | 50 means no change |

### Release times (ET, as of 2026)

| Release | Time |
|---|---|
| CPI, PPI, jobs report, GDP, PCE, retail sales, weekly claims (Thursday) | 8:30 a.m. |

| ISM PMIs (manufacturing: 1st business day, services: 3rd), JOLTS | 10:00 a.m. |
| FOMC statement | 2:00 p.m. |
| Fed balance sheet, H.4.1 (Thursday) | 4:30 p.m. |

### Who publishes what

| Publisher | Releases |
|---|---|
| BLS | CPI, PPI, Employment Situation, JOLTS |
| BEA | GDP (advance, second, third estimates), PCE |
| Census Bureau | Retail sales |
| Federal Reserve | FOMC statement, SEP, minutes (3 weeks later), H.4.1 |
| ISM, S&P Global | PMIs |

### Rules

- The surprise moves prices, not the number. Compare with the consensus, then read the details and the revisions.
- Payrolls come from the establishment survey, the unemployment rate from the household survey.
- The Fed targets 2% inflation in PCE. Traders see CPI first.
- GDP has three estimates, and the average advance-to-third revision is 0.6 percentage points (BEA, 1996 to 2025).
- The sign of the stock reaction to strong data depends on the regime: inflation worry means good news is bad news.
- Sector rotation by cycle phase is a loose pattern, not a rule.

### Before each high-importance release

1. Is the time in ET, and checked on the agency page?
2. Do I know the consensus and the prior value?
3. Do I have a written rule for my open positions?
4. Does the rule fit my trading plan limits?
