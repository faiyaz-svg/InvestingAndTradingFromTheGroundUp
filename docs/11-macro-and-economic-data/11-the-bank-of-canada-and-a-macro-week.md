## The idea

You hold US stocks in a Canadian account, and you convert between Canadian and US dollars. That means two central banks affect your account: the Fed through US prices, and the Bank of Canada through the exchange rate and through Canadian rates.

The **Bank of Canada** (BoC) is Canada's central bank. It normally conducts monetary policy by changing its **target for the overnight rate**, also called the key policy interest rate ([Bank of Canada, monetary policy](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/core-functions/monetary-policy/), as of 2026). The target for inflation is 2%, the midpoint of a 1% to 3% control range, set jointly with the federal government and reviewed every five years. The BoC bases rate decisions on where it judges inflation will be six to eight quarters ahead, as well as where it is now (same page).

## How it works

### The schedule

The BoC announces its rate decisions on fixed dates, eight times a year, and holds a press conference after each ([Bank of Canada key interest rate page](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/core-functions/monetary-policy/key-interest-rate/)). Announcements are at 9:45 a.m. ET ([Bank of Canada upcoming events](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/press/upcoming-events/)). The Monetary Policy Report (MPR), the full update of the Bank's outlook for the economy and inflation, comes with four of the eight decisions. The 2026 dates (as of 2026), with the MPR dates marked:

| Date | MPR |
|---|---|
| January 28 | Yes |
| March 18 | No |
| April 29 | Yes |
| June 10 | No |
| July 15 | Yes |
| September 2 | No |
| October 28 | Yes |
| December 9 | No |

Compare the Fed: also eight meetings a year, with an SEP at four of them (lesson 2). In 2026, five Bank of Canada decision days fall on the last day of an FOMC meeting: January 28, March 18, April 29, October 28 and December 9. Statistics Canada publishes Canada's CPI and Labour Force Survey, the counterparts of the BLS reports.

### How a gap between the two banks moves USD/CAD

**USD/CAD** is the number of Canadian dollars one US dollar buys. When investors expect US rates to be higher than Canadian rates, US dollar assets pay more, and demand for US dollars tends to rise, which tends to raise USD/CAD. The key input is the expected gap in policy rates, not the current gap. Other things move USD/CAD too, including oil prices and risk appetite. Part 17 covers currencies ([What moves currencies](../17-commodities-forex-and-reits/05-what-moves-currencies.md)).

### Worked example: a hot US print widens the gap

Made-up numbers. The Fed range is 3.50% to 3.75%, so its middle is 3.625%. The BoC target is 2.75%, and the market expects no BoC change.

Before the US CPI release, the market gives a 70% chance of a 25 bp Fed cut at the next meeting. After the hot print, it gives 30% (the odds from lesson 8).

1. Expected Fed rate before: 3.625 - 0.25 x 0.70 = 3.45%.
2. Expected Fed rate after: 3.625 - 0.25 x 0.30 = 3.55%.
3. Expected BoC rate: 2.75% both times.
4. Expected gap before: 3.45 - 2.75 = 0.70 percentage points. After: 3.55 - 2.75 = 0.80 percentage points.

The gap in favour of US dollar assets widened by 0.10 percentage points, which points the same way as the USD/CAD rise in lesson 8. The size of the move in the exchange rate depends on much more than this gap.

</div>

For your account, a stronger US dollar raises the Canadian-dollar value of US stocks you hold, and a weaker one lowers it ([Currency risk on US stocks](../02-accounts-currency-and-taxes/05-currency-risk-on-us-stocks.md)).

## A macro week

Here is the routine for a week, using the real schedule for December 1 to 9, 2026 (the dates are on the agency pages, as of November 2026). Any outcomes below are made up.

1. Sunday: build the calendar (lesson 7). The week holds ISM Manufacturing on Tuesday at 10:00 a.m. ET, jobless claims and ISM Services on Thursday, the jobs report on Friday at 8:30, then the FOMC meeting on December 8 to 9 and the Bank of Canada on Wednesday, December 9 at 9:45 a.m. ET, with the FOMC statement at 2:00 p.m. ET the same day. Rank: jobs report high, FOMC and BoC days high, the others medium or low.
2. Write a rule for each high item (lesson 7).
3. Before each release, note the consensus. After it, write the surprise (lesson 1) and the revisions.
4. Note what happened to the rate path: did fed funds futures move (lessons 2 and 8)?
5. Note the move in the 2-year yield, the dollar and your own positions.
6. On Friday, write one paragraph in the journal: what surprised, what the market did and whether your rule held.

A filled-in line for Friday, with made-up numbers: payrolls consensus +150,000, actual +190,000, so S = +40,000. Prior months revised by -30,000, so the net is +10,000. Unemployment rate 4.3% against 4.2% expected, +0.1 percentage points. The rate path barely moved, because the details offset.

## Part check

1\. Which agency publishes CPI, which GDP, and which the retail sales report?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

CPI: BLS. GDP: BEA. Retail sales: the Census Bureau.

</details>

2\. A futures price of 96.50 and a range of 3.50% to 3.75%. What are the implied odds of a 25 bp cut?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Implied rate 3.50%. p = (3.625 - 3.50) / 0.25 = 50%.

</details>

3\. Stock A pays US$20 a year for 5 years and Stock B pays US$100 in year 10. Which is more sensitive to a rise in the discount rate, and why?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Stock B, because its cash is further away, so the divisor (1+r)^t changes more with r.

</details>

4\. Core CPI consensus is +0.3% m/m and the actual is +0.4%. Give the surprise and the likely direction of yields.

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

+0.1 percentage points. Yields are likely to rise, as expected rates rise.

</details>

5\. Which survey gives the unemployment rate, and which gives payrolls?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Unemployment rate: the household survey. Payrolls: the establishment survey.

</details>

6\. At what times ET do these come out: CPI, ISM PMIs, the FOMC statement, the Bank of Canada decision?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

CPI at 8:30 a.m., ISM PMIs at 10:00 a.m., the FOMC statement at 2:00 p.m. and the Bank of Canada decision at 9:45 a.m.

</details>

7\. Which inflation measure does the Fed target, at what level?

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The PCE price index, at 2 percent over the longer run.

</details>

8\. Give one reason sector rotation rules are loose.

<details class="solution" markdown>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Any one of: the cycle phase is dated after the fact, each cycle has different causes, sector contents change, or prices move before the data does.

</details>

## Key terms

- **Bank of Canada (BoC)**: Canada's central bank.
- **Target for the overnight rate**: the Bank of Canada's key policy interest rate.
- **Monetary Policy Report (MPR)**: the Bank of Canada's full outlook, published four times a year.
- **USD/CAD**: the number of Canadian dollars one US dollar buys.
- **Rate differential**: the gap between expected policy rates in two countries.
