## Cheat sheet

### Formulas

| Item | Formula | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Futures hedge price | physical price + futures gain per barrel | A short hedge locks the price at which the futures were sold, before basis |
| Oil contract move | \( \Delta P \times 1{,}000 \) | US$ per WTI contract (1,000 barrels) |
| Cost of holding gold | real yield \( \times \) gold price | US$ a year per ounce, against holding a TIPS |
| Fund value in contango | \( V_{12} = V_0 \times \frac{S_{12}}{S_0} \times (1+c)^{-12} \) | \(c\) = monthly contango; simplified, fixed \(c\) |
| Pair price | USD/CAD 1.3650 | 1 US$ costs C$1.3650; inverse is 1 / 1.3650 |
| Pip value | \( \text{units} \times 0.0001 \) | In the quote currency; divide by the rate for US$. Yen pairs use 0.01 |
| Forward rate | \( F = S \times \frac{1+i_{quote}}{1+i_{base}} \) | One year; for \(T\) years use \(1 + iT\) |
| Leverage from margin | \( 1 / \text{margin rate} \) | Margin rate is CIRO's list or your broker's, whichever is higher |
| FFO | \( NI + D - G \) | Net income + real estate depreciation - gains on property sales (Nareit) |
| AFFO | FFO - recurring capex - non-cash items | Defined by each REIT; read the reconciliation |
| Payout ratio | dividend / AFFO per share | Also quoted on FFO |
| Cap rate | \( NOI / V \) | Yearly yield on the property before debt |
| NAV | \( NOI / \text{cap rate} - \text{debt} \) | Often per share |
| US REIT payout test | at least 90% of REIT taxable income | IRS Form 1120-REIT instructions |

### Rules to keep

- A commodity futures price is not the spot price. In contango a fund that rolls loses roll yield, even when spot is flat.
- Oil and gold news is judged against consensus. Inventory reports (EIA, Wednesdays 10:30 a.m. ET) move price on the surprise.
- The higher-rate currency trades at a forward discount. A carry trade breaks even at the forward rate.
- Foreign cash you hold is already a forex position.
- Net income misleads for a REIT. Use FFO and AFFO, and check each REIT's definition.
- A cap rate rise cuts equity value by more than property value, because debt is fixed.

### Checklist for a commodity, currency or REIT position

1. Which instrument is it: futures, a fund that rolls, a fund that holds the metal, a currency pair, REIT units?
2. For futures or a fund: the shape of the curve and the roll schedule.
3. For a pair: base and quote, pip value for your size, the spread, the margin rate and the stop distance in pips.
4. For a REIT: FFO and AFFO per share, payout ratio, debt maturities, cap rate and NAV, and the 10-year yield.
5. The calendar: EIA report, central bank dates, earnings release.
