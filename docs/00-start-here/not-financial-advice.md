# Not financial advice

This book is for education. It teaches how markets, instruments and methods work, and it shows how to check the numbers. It does not tell you what to buy or sell, and it does not take your own situation into account.

## What that means in practice

- Trade examples use made-up stocks, such as "Stock A", and made-up price data. A made-up example shows how a method works. It does not predict anything.
- A real stock, ETF or coin is never called a good or a bad buy. Real tickers appear only for real facts, such as a filing or an index.
- Trading can lose money. With margin, short selling, futures and some option strategies, you can lose more than you put in.
- The author is not a registered adviser, and nothing here is financial, tax or legal advice.
- Rules, fees, limits and tax treatment change. Each lesson gives its source and the year, so check the current source before you act on a number. For tax questions that affect you, use the CRA or a qualified tax professional.

## Checking a firm or adviser in Canada

If you deal with a firm or a person who gives advice or sells investments, check that they are registered. Two free tools, in this order:

1. The [CSA National Registration Search](https://info.securities-administrators.ca/nrsmobile/nrssearch.aspx?lang=EN) covers registered firms and individuals across Canada. It shows their registration categories and any terms and conditions. Registration conditions for Quebec are not available there.
2. If the search shows a registration with an investment dealer, look the adviser up in CIRO's [AdvisorReport](https://www.ciro.ca/advisor-report-search). It shows regulatory status and background for advisers at CIRO-regulated firms.

Provincial regulators such as the [OSC](https://osc.gov.on.ca/en/Dealers_registrant-search_index.htm) also have registrant searches. For disciplinary history, CIRO and the CSA publish their own lists, and the CIRO and CSA pages link to them.

Registration does not mean a recommendation is right for you. It means the person or firm is allowed to do the work.
