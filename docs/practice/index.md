# Practice

The book has three practice tools. Each one turns a lesson into something you do, and each one fits the practice block of the [30-minute routine](../00-start-here/the-30-minute-routine.md).

## Chart drills

[Chart drills](chart-drills.md) show a chart and ask a question about it. For some questions you click the chart to mark a price, and for others you type a number or pick an answer. All the price data is made up. Cards you miss come back sooner, and your progress stays in your browser. A drill can run at any time, so it suits the 10-minute practice block when the market is shut.

## Paper trading

[Paper trading](paper-trading.md) places trades with fake money at real prices. It shows whether you can follow your rules, not whether a plan makes money. The page sets the paper blocks the book asks for and says when to go live. Trades on an intraday setup need the market open, so keep them for a session between 9:30 a.m. and 4 p.m. ET.

## Trade journal

The [trade journal](trade-journal.md) records every trade so you can measure results and review decisions. The page lists what to record, the formulas and the weekly review, with a spreadsheet and a CSV to download. Once a week, replace one lesson with a 30 minute review of the journal, or of your paper trades if you have no live ones.

## How they fit the routine

The routine splits 30 minutes into blocks, and the last 10 minutes are practice. Pick the tool that matches the lesson: a chart drill after a charting lesson, a paper trade after a setup, a journal entry after a trade. See [The 30-minute routine](../00-start-here/the-30-minute-routine.md) for the full split and for how to fit it around market hours.
