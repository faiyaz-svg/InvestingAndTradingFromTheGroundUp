# Chart drills

These drills show a chart and ask a question about it. For some questions you click the chart to mark a price. For others you type a number or pick an answer. All the price data is made up, so no real stock is involved.

Cards you miss come back sooner. Cards you get right come back later. Your progress is kept in this browser only.
